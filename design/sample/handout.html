1. What is photosynthesis?

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make their own food. They use light energy from the sun, water from the soil and carbon dioxide from the air. The food made is glucose, and oxygen is given off.

2. Word equation

carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen

This reaction needs light and chlorophyll.

3. Where it happens

Photosynthesis takes place mainly in the leaves, inside tiny parts called chloroplasts. Chloroplasts contain chlorophyll, the green pigment that absorbs light.

4. Factors that affect the rate

  • Light intensity
  • Carbon dioxide concentration
  • Temperature
